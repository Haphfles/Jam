NOT A COMPETITION. A CONNECTION.

A band with an open door.

Anyone can listen. Five people can play at a time, with one spot per instrument. When the stage is full, join the queue: the next open instrument goes to the first person waiting.

Use your keyboard or tap the instrument. For phone control, take a spot and select “Use your phone.” Scan the QR code and play on your phone while your computer makes the sound.

Everything is tuned to C minor pentatonic, so it’s easy to find a groove together. This is a free-form jam: there’s no score and no backing track.

Sound starts only after you enable it. Network delay varies; this is a casual jam space. Phone pairing uses a web link, not Bluetooth. A disconnected spot is released after about 15 seconds.
